What is a Prompt?

The instruction you give an AI model to produce a response.

🎯

Role, Context, Task

Telling the AI who to be, what background it has, and what to do.

🧱

Constraints

Limits like length, tone, or audience level shape better answers.

📐

Output Format

Specifying format (list, table, JSON) improves usability.

0️⃣

Zero-shot

Asking the AI to perform a task with no examples given.

🔢

Few-shot

Providing a few examples to guide the AI's response style.
